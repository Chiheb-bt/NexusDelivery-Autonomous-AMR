Step-by-Step Experience

From Order Dispatch to Doorstep Unlocking

A seamless four-stage autonomous delivery cycle validated from simulation to physical robot integration.

1

Doorstep Pin Drop

Customer chooses a destination on the verified Gazebo city vector map. The geocoder validates reachability and snaps to the nearest sidewalk curb.

2

Intelligent Dispatch

The backend verifies battery reserve and kinematics, plans an obstacle-free A* route around the Central Depot, and generates a secure 6-digit OTP.

3

Vision Corridor Transit

The robot follows designated pedestrian sidewalks, tracking actors at 30 FPS and maintaining a 10-state safety supervisor with dynamic TTC deceleration.

4

Contactless Handover

Upon curbside arrival, the customer enters the 6-digit OTP code. The motorized hatch rotates to 60°, and the customer retrieves their fresh package.

Technology Architecture

Rigorous Robotics Engineering • Zero Mock Telemetry

Built on ROS 2 Humble Hawksbill, Gazebo Classic 11.10.2, and embedded Linux edge compute.

👁️

Active IR Stereo RGB-D Vision

Intel RealSense D435i optical configuration providing dense depth pointclouds (0.28m to 5.0m range), vectorized ground-plane filtering, and pedestrian corridor analysis.

🧠

Hailo-8L Edge AI Acceleration

Dedicated M.2 AI coprocessor delivering 13 INT8 TOPS at 2.5W TDP, running YOLOv8n object detection at 138.4 FPS (7.18ms latency) for real-time collision avoidance.

🛡️

10-State Deterministic Safety FSM

Predictive multi-zone safety supervisor enforcing corridor clearance, sensor timeout watchdogs, traffic signal stops, and latched emergency braking states.
