**Auditor:** Autonomous Systems Engineering Lead  
**Classification Schema:**
- **[Cat A] Genuine Simulation Capture:** Authentic recording/frame from Gazebo Classic running the project's URDF/world.
- **[Cat B] Genuine Software Screenshot / Telemetry:** Direct capture of ROS 2 computational graph, RQT, database, or web portal UI.
- **[Cat C] CAD / Engineering Drawing:** Technical schematic, PCB layout, wiring diagram, or 3D mechanical model.
- **[Cat D] AI-Generated Concept Image:** Synthetic image generated by generative diffusion/AI models.
- **[Cat E] Generic Stock Image:** Third-party stock photography of unrelated commercial hardware.
- **[Cat F] Obsolete / Placeholder:** Deprecated screenshots representing previous iterations or unverified states.
